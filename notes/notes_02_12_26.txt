Deep reinforcement learning
there are two ways to approach problems, either with search or optimization
search is the forward view
    - it is value maxing
    - examples include Monte carlo, TD(n) Q learning, and tree based
    - has great solution gaurantees
    - permformance improves with variance reductions
optimization is the backwards view
    - Explanation for maximizing credit-assignment
    - examples TD(lambda), representation learning, and deep learning
    - realted to sochastic gradient descent, regression, feature learning, PCA analysis, Fourier methods and NN optimization
    - medium solution gaurantees
    - great convergnece rates

The curse of dimensionality: Usable value and policy updates do not scale with states and actions

For DL
    - Given a policy pim we are trying to determine:
        - The true value function
        - the true Q value
        - or a policy updates
    - so we need a really good function approximator
    - the trick is to "categorize states/actions" by a fixed number of relevence

We consider a representation "really good" if the features are "independent, sufficient and minimal"

features are functions of measurable data, usually they are either compressions or critical notions
You can describe a state by its features
The notions of deep learning comes form the fact that you can iterate this process by making feature space your new scaling space
