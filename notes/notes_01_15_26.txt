Reward Shaping
- Without actions, accruing rewards is random
-Tips:
    1. Use small regative rewards everywhere to later encourage an impatience to explore
    2. Use small positive rewards everywhere to later encouage caution and vigilance
    3. Use relatively large positive rewards only for the goal/terminal state, unless you have a very specific reason not to.
    4. Use relatively large negateive rewards for locally bad states, like dangerous situations
    5. Infinites and negative Infinites are sometimes useful for visualizing obstacles, but they must always be self contained.
