Partial Observability
Everything weve done so far is basedof off states
    - this is not always realistic
        - works well for board games and simulations in completely nown environments
        - does not work for things with visual data, card games with hidden hand, basically anything with partial Observability
        - multi agents is a good example as the agents can only know what each other are doing through communication which can be though of as observation and not a state

State vs Obs Example:
    A robot thats needs to move through and environment

    State:
        - exact position
        - full room map
        - positions of obstacles
    Obs:
        - RGB image
        - IMU reading
        - LiDAR sweep

- The same state can produce many different observations
- The same observation is consistent with many different states
- multiple measurint instruments can be aggregated into one observation

Beleifs:
    - from the history of the model we can make a prob distro of what state it thinks its in
    - satisfy the markov property

Bayes Theorem
    - the probability of an event, based on prior knowledge of conditions that might be related to the event
        - the probability of something given the probabilities of a bunch of other prequisite knowledge
            i.e. the prop that a illness test is true positive given the success rates of previous tests, the odds of getting the disease in the first place, etc...
    - emphasizes the importance of understanding how priors effect things

Fisher information
